Our Story

Our Legacy

From Murali Enterprises, established in 1985 by Mr. K. V. Muralidharan, to Udhayam Constructions & Property Developers, we continue to build upon a legacy of experience and trust while serving the evolving construction and property development needs of Chennai and its surrounding areas.

Over four decades, we have grown from a trusted contracting firm into a comprehensive property solutions company — offering construction, development, valuation and real estate services under one roof.

Our Founder

A Legacy Built on Dedication

Udhayam Constructions & Property Developers traces its roots to the vision and dedication of Mr. K. V. Muralidharan, who established Murali Enterprises in 1985 in Chennai.

What began as a focused construction and contracting enterprise has grown, over four decades, into a comprehensive property solutions company covering construction, development, valuation and real estate services.

The foundational values instilled by Mr. Muralidharan — quality, integrity, and a commitment to timely execution — remain the guiding principles of Udhayam Constructions today.
